Why it helps

“The municipality could help” is not yet actionable. Students need a real organisation, a public contact, a reason to approach them and one clear thing to ask for.

The platform’s role
ActHub does not need to “solve” the issue for the student. Its job is to remove the friction between “someone should help us” and “we know exactly who we can contact today and what to ask.”


You are not supposed to know the whole system already. Finding the right organisation — and making a respectful first contact — is part of taking action.
