Action Pathway - a method developed to enable success stories of local actions globally The AP method is built upon existing research and theories of action competence for sustainability, an ability that CAN BE developed no matter the conditions. We believe that action competence is the key to not only human well-being but also planetary well-being. Research proves that action competence is essential to bring about not only positive change but also to live a happier and more fulfilling life. Humans need purpose, and we are here to support you in taking meaningful steps towards well-being. Our approach We are taking an eco-social approach to valuing life on Earth.
